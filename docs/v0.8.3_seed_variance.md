# The energy×geometry "seed variance" — WITHDRAWN, and what is actually there

**Status 13/08/2026: the central claim of this document (versions before today)
was wrong.** It reported that the detector-relevant energy ratio spans
0.455–1.222 across training seeds and called that a training-stochasticity
systematic worth quoting in the paper. That spread is an **artefact of the
metric**, which on this source measures the species mix rather than the energy
conditional.

The original text is kept below §5 as a record, because the v0.8.3 models were
trained and their data is real. The corrected reading is §1–§4.

**Decision unchanged: stay on v0.8.2 for the validation and the paper.** What
changes is the reason — not "quote a large seed systematic", but "there is no
demonstrated large seed systematic, and the metric that suggested one was
mismeasuring."

---

## 1. What went wrong with the metric

`energy_vs_impact_parameter` compares the **median generated energy** to the
median real energy among crossings aimed at the detector. On a single-species,
unimodal source that is a sound estimator of the energy conditional. The CR
crossing population is neither.

At b < 10 mm the real population is:

| species | fraction | median energy |
|---|---|---|
| `mu-` | 48.008 % | 828.373 MeV |
| `gamma` | 49.789 % | 0.511 MeV |
| `e-` | 2.092 % | 3.170 MeV |
| **aggregate** | | **13.288 MeV** |

Two populations three orders of magnitude apart, split almost exactly 50/50, and
**disjoint**: the gamma 99th percentile is 30.5 MeV and the `mu-` 1st percentile
is 24.1 MeV. The 50th percentile of the combined sample lands in the gap between
them, where there is almost no probability density. The aggregate median is
consequently close to a *step function* of the muon fraction — the worst possible
configuration for a median statistic.

## 2. Proof, using only the real data

Reweight the species mix of the real b < 10 mm crossings. Leave every energy
value untouched. There is by construction zero energy error:

| `mu-` reweight | `mu-` fraction | aggregate median | ratio to truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.955 | 46.834 % | 9.287 MeV | **0.699** |
| 0.980 | 47.686 % | 11.754 MeV | 0.885 |
| 1.000 | 47.953 % | 13.172 MeV | 0.991 |
| 1.012 | 48.325 % | 14.982 MeV | **1.128** |
| 1.050 | 49.268 % | 21.316 MeV | 1.604 |

A ±5 % shift in one species fraction moves the metric across 0.70–1.60. The
measured per-seed muon reweights at b < 10 mm are **1.012 / 1.012 / 0.955** —
precisely this range, and precisely the seeds that produced the "0.495–1.222
spread."

Reproduce with `tools/species_resolved_evsb.py`.

## 3. What the model actually does

Same three v0.8.2 CR checkpoints, 2e6 generated each, neutrinos filtered as
`generation/export.py` does, metric computed **per species**:

### gamma — exact
| cut | seed42 | seed7 | seed13 |
|---|---|---|---|
| all ingoing | 1.001 | 1.001 | 1.001 |
| b < 50 mm | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.029 |
| b < 20 mm | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| b < 10 mm | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

### `mu-` — within ±6 %
| cut | seed42 | seed7 | seed13 |
|---|---|---|---|
| all ingoing | 0.956 | 1.043 | 0.964 |
| b < 50 mm | 0.960 | 1.063 | 0.947 |
| b < 20 mm | 0.962 | 1.064 | 0.958 |
| b < 10 mm | 1.003 | 1.055 | 0.976 |

### `e-` — scattered, but statistics-limited
0.555–1.303 across seeds and cuts. `e-` is 1.9–2.1 % of the population and there
are **639 real events** at b < 10 mm. Not a demonstrated defect.

**The energy conditional is not broken.** Gammas are reproduced exactly at every
cut and every seed; muons to ±6 %.

## 4. The real effect, and what remains unmeasured

There *is* a seed-dependent error, an order of magnitude smaller than claimed and
in a different channel — the **species mix conditional on direction**:

| cut | species | seed42 | seed7 | seed13 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| b < 10 mm | `mu-` | 1.012 | 1.012 | 0.955 |
| b < 10 mm | gamma | 0.998 | 1.001 | 1.039 |
| b < 10 mm | `e-` | 0.760 | 0.702 | 1.085 |
| all ingoing | `mu-` | 1.015 | 1.012 | 0.992 |

Global species fractions are correct to 0.3 % (γ 1.003, e− 1.002, μ− 0.997 on
DM1.2). The error appears only once you condition on pointing at the detector —
which is exactly the class of joint-distribution failure marginal validation
cannot see, just at ~±5 % rather than a factor 2.5.

The same signature appears independently on **DM1.2**: among aimed crossings MAGI
runs +8.8 % muons, −13.6 % `e-`, −7.7 % gammas at b < 10 mm, while per-species
energies are right (muons 0.99–1.03). There it produces a measurable ~4 %
downward shift in deposited energy, traced to incidence angle — median sec θ
0.955 of truth against a deposit-median ratio of 0.960.

**Never measured:** a three-seed *detector-level* comparison on CR. Every seed
claim in this document, old and new, comes from crossing-level statistics. The
only detector-level multi-seed result that exists is the DM1.2 3-seed ensemble:
**0.970 ± 0.032** overall, **0.999 ± 0.037** in the MIP band. Until a CR
equivalent is run, no seed systematic should be quoted in either direction.

## 5. Revised plan for the next version

The old §6.1 targeted the **energy conditional** — importance weighting on `b`,
conditional-moment penalties on mean log E, rank-correlation penalties on
logE-vs-`b`. §3 shows that term is not the problem, so **that plan is aimed at
the wrong quantity and should not be implemented as written.**

Replacing it, in order:

1. **Fix the metric first.** Make `energy_vs_impact_parameter` species-resolved
   by default and refuse to return an aggregate on a multi-species input. A
   metric that swings 0.70–1.60 on zero energy error is worse than no metric —
   it cost this project a wrong conclusion and a v0.8.3 development cycle.
2. **Target the aimed species mix.** The quantity that is actually off is
   `P(species | direction, position)`. The type head is conditioned but the
   sampled type is drawn from a global `type_probs`, so any direction-dependence
   has to arrive through `z` alone. Conditioning the type draw on the sampled
   geometry — the structural analogue of what `energy_condition_geometry` did
   for energy — is the direct fix.
3. **Measure the detector-level seed spread** by injecting three CR checkpoints
   into Geant4 at matched N, as was done on DM1.2. This is the number the paper
   actually needs and it has never been taken.
4. **`e-` statistics.** Whether the `e-` scatter is real needs more generated
   events at small `b`, not more seeds.

Retained from the old plan, still valid regardless:

- Neutrinos must be filtered exactly as `export.py` does in any generated-vs-real
  comparison — a mistake that bit during the original study and inflated the
  10–100 MeV decade from 6.4 % to 10.6 %.
- Matched N with bootstrap CIs; small-`b` bins are statistics-limited.
- `energy_condition_geometry` stays in `core/model.py` defaulting to `False`, so
  v0.8.2 is bit-identical and the experiment is resumable.

## 6. What to quote in the paper

- The CR normalization-free result **0.848 ± 0.125** (§4b of the validation
  note) — a real Geant4 detector-level measurement, unaffected by any of this.
- The DM1.2 iso result **0.970 ± 0.032** overall, **0.999 ± 0.037** MIP band,
  from a 3-seed ensemble, with 100 % crossing coverage after the overlap fix.
- The K-40 BuildingModel result **1.118 ± 0.373**, KS p = 0.60.
- **No seed systematic.** It is not established in either direction.

## Appendix — superseded record (v0.8.3 experiment, 12/08/2026)

Kept because the models were trained and the data is real; the *interpretation*
below is invalidated by §1–§3, since every number in it is the aggregate metric.

v0.8.3 = `energy_condition_geometry=True`, `latent_dim` 8→12.

| cut | v0.8.2 42/7/13 | v0.8.3 42/7/13 |
|---|---|---|
| all ingoing | 1.290 / 1.272 / 0.813 | 1.006 / 0.822 / 0.918 |
| b < 50 mm | 2.031 / 1.615 / 0.696 | 1.178 / 0.813 / 1.109 |
| b < 20 mm | 1.568 / 1.466 / 0.527 | 1.213 / 0.543 / 0.958 |
| b < 10 mm | 1.222 / 1.057 / 0.495 | 1.082 / 0.455 / 0.962 |
| b < 5 mm | 1.058 / 0.841 / 0.508 | 0.665 / 0.439 / 0.625 |

`seed13` of v0.8.2 is the checkpoint behind `MAGI_validation_note.md` §4b.

v0.8.3 did regress the two lowest energy decades consistently across all three
seeds (0.001–0.01 MeV: 0.880/0.921/0.995 → 0.677/0.715/0.738; 0.01–0.1 MeV:
1.686/1.296/1.767 → 2.216/1.714/1.979). That regression is measured on
*marginals* and therefore stands — it remains a reason not to adopt v0.8.3 as
implemented.

The seed-ensembling measurement (3 seeds pooled at fixed total N, giving
0.898 [0.765, 1.068] at b < 10 mm against single seeds 0.495–1.222) is also an
aggregate-metric number. Pooling reduced the *mix* variance; it says nothing
about energy. Ensembling remains reasonable practice — the DM1.2 production run
used it — but the "±15 % after ensembling vs ±40 % single-seed" claim is
withdrawn.
