_Written 2026-07-29, branch `energy-mixture`, after the Phase 3 / Phase 4 work of

[`v0.8.1_improvement_plan.md`](v0.8.1_improvement_plan.md) §6 and reorders
[`v0.8.2_plan.md`](v0.8.2_plan.md) §6; both remain valid for their per-item

## 0. What this document is

Three things, in one place:

1. **An index of every document produced so far**, with what each one is still
   authoritative for and what has been superseded (§1).
2. **An honest statement of where v0.8.1 stands** against the beta bar, with
   every number traceable to the file that produced it (§2–§3).
3. **The remaining plan** to either clear the bar or call the fallback, with the
   v0.8.2 backlog folded in (§5–§8).

The short version: the **architecture is validated and stable**; the **line
intensity calibration is neither**. Two tuning levers have been tried and both
returned null results at the noise level. Before spending more training runs we
need a *mechanism*, and there are two concrete candidates neither of which has
been tested. There is also a measurement gap that undermines the whole
comparison: **v0.7.2 has never been scored on the same axes as v0.8.**

## 2. Where v0.8.1 actually stands

All numbers below are **mean ± std over 3 seeds (42 / 7 / 13)**, produced by
`tools/acceptance_v0_8.py --seeds 42 7 13`, the multi-seed harness built
2026-07-29. Checkpoints: `trained_models/v0_8_1_cuka2_CR{,_seed7,_seed13}` and
`trained_models/v0_8_1_Small{,_seed7,_seed13}`.

### 2.1 What is stable and passing

| Metric | CR | Small | Bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coupling max\|Δcorr\| | **0.0345 ± 0.0048** | **0.0357 ± 0.0102** | ≤0.05 ✅ |
| Global Wasserstein, log₁₀E | **0.0227 ± 0.0042** | **0.0065 ± 0.0006** | ≤0.05 ✅ |

**This is v0.8's actual scientific contribution and it holds up with error
bars.** The conditional prior reproduces every real cross-correlation over
(logE, u_r, u_v, φ_r, φ_v) to within the bar, on both sources, across seeds.
The theory for why this was needed at all is in
[`v0.8_learnable_prior_theory.md`](v0.8_learnable_prior_theory.md) §2–§3.

### 2.2 What is failing

| Line | Real fraction | Recovery (mean ± std) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| CR e+e− 511 | 1.52e-2 | 1.677 ± 0.426 | over |
| CR Al Kα1 | 3.10e-3 | 0.702 ± 0.290 | under |
| CR Cu Kα1 | 1.05e-3 | (routing 0.688 ± 0.191) | under |
| CR Cu Kβ | 4.20e-4 | 1.166 ± 0.224 | borderline |
| Small e+e− 511 | 3.95e-3 | 1.232 ± 1.072 | **σ = 87% of mean** |
| Small Al Kα2 | 5.44e-5 | 3.412 ± 0.376 | far over |
| Small Cu Kα1 | 3.31e-5 | (routing 4.903 ± 0.356) | far over |

**0 of 3 seeds pass overall, on either source.**

Two distinct problems, and they need separating:

- **Accuracy.** Intensities are wrong by 0.69× to 4.9×.
- **Stability.** σ/μ runs 25–87%. Even where a mean landed in band, individual
  runs would scatter out of it. For a beta default — which a user trains on
  their own data with their own seed, unattended — this is the harder blocker.

### 2.3 What is known-unreliable in the table above

`near_line_continuum_ratios` in [`../tools/acceptance_v0_8.py`](../tools/acceptance_v0_8.py)
still has the side-band blind spot that was fixed in
`compute_line_integral_recovery` ([`v0.8.1_line_truth.md`](v0.8.1_line_truth.md)
§9), and it is **not** gated by that function's guards. For closely-spaced lines
— CR's Cu Kα1/Kα2 at 21 eV, Al Kα1/Kα2 at 0.5 eV — its numbers are not
trustworthy. It currently reports failure on nearly every line on both sources;
that row should be discounted, not counted as independent evidence, until it is
fixed. **Fixing it is a Phase A task.**

The line-recovery failures in §2.2 are computed with the *corrected* metric and
stand on their own regardless.

### 2.4 Negative results already banked — do not re-try without new information

| Attempt | Outcome | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Per-line `gate_class_weights`, calibrated from measured routing | No effect distinguishable from noise; CR's untouched **control** line moved more (+27%) than either weighted line; response exponent spanned 15× | [`v0.8.1_line_truth.md`](v0.8.1_line_truth.md) §10 |
| Continuum polish: CDF-warp knot boost + `continuum_flow_bins` 24→32 | Combined run degraded every CR band; split runs showed **neither lever alone** reproduced it; 3-seed grid put every difference under **1.2σ** of combined noise | [`v0.8.1_line_truth.md`](v0.8.1_line_truth.md) §11–§11.3 |

The methodological lesson from both, and the reason the multi-seed harness now
exists: **CR's per-band Wasserstein has σ/μ of 40–50%**, larger than most effect
sizes worth chasing. Small's is much tighter at ~14% ([§11.4](v0.8.1_line_truth.md)).
Any future claim needs ≥3 seeds *and* a held-out control.

## 3. Why the current go/no-go bar cannot be used as written

[`v0.8.1_improvement_plan.md`](v0.8.1_improvement_plan.md) §5 states:

> CR Al, Ni, 511 recovery ≥ 0.8 and Cu Kβ ≤ 1.2; between-line continuum ≥ 0.85;
> CR Compton-edge Wasserstein ≤ v0.7.2's; all coupling residuals ≤ ±0.05;
> multi-seed std small.

Four problems:

1. **"Ni Kβ" does not exist.** That peak was CR's Cu Kα, mislabelled by the old
   greedy matcher ([`v0.8.1_line_truth.md`](v0.8.1_line_truth.md) §3.1).
2. **It is one-sided.** Written when everything looked under-generated. Post-fix
   the dominant failure is *over*-generation, which "≥ 0.8" does not catch — CR's
   511 at 1.677 technically passes.
3. **"multi-seed std small" is not a number.** We now have the data to make it one.
4. **"≤ v0.7.2's" is unmeasurable today.** See §5.1.

## 4. The restated bar

| Criterion | Threshold | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Per-line recovery | **[0.8, 1.2]**, two-sided | Only for lines above the detection floor below |
| Per-line stability | **σ ≤ 0.15 over ≥3 seeds** | So a user's single unattended run likely lands in band |
| Detection floor | **≥5σ** Poisson significance | Particle-physics discovery convention; below-floor lines are *not modelled*, and their absence is a correct result, not a failure |
| Near-line continuum | ≥0.85 | **Only after the §2.3 metric bug is fixed** |
| Energy marginal | global Wasserstein ≤ v0.7.2's, measured on the same harness | Requires §5.1 |
| Coupling | max\|Δcorr\| ≤ 0.05 | Currently passing on both sources |

Rationale for 5σ over an arbitrary count: it is the standard discovery threshold
in the field, it is defensible in the paper without tuning, and it replaces the
`count >= 100` rule that [`v0.8.2_plan.md`](v0.8.2_plan.md) §5 already identified
as dataset-size-dependent.

## 5. Two gaps that reframe the remaining work

### 5.1 v0.7.2 has never been measured on the same axes

> **✅ Measured 2026-07-29 — Phase A1.** New `tools/score_v0_7_2.py`; full
> results in [`v0.8.1_line_truth.md`](v0.8.1_line_truth.md) §13.1,
> `docs/_data/v072_acceptance.json`. Headline: **v0.7.2 beats v0.8.1 on the
> global Wasserstein on both sources** (CR 0.0141 vs 0.0227±0.0042; Small
> 0.0024 vs 0.0065±0.0006) — the §10 risk register's "Low-Medium likelihood"
> risk materialized. v0.8.1 wins coupling by 5-6× (confirming v0.7.2's was
> never verified and is in fact bad) and, decisively, v0.7.2's 511 keV line
> recovered **0.000** — not "small", zero generated events landed in the
> ±8.5 eV window against 52,225 real (CR) — because a categorical head
> sampled uniformly within a ~20 keV bin cannot produce a detector-resolution
> line at all. The v0.7.2 Small checkpoint also turned out to have been
> trained on a restricted energy window (`e_min_cut=0.05`, `e_max_cut=0.8`
> MeV) that excludes every fluorescence line below 50 keV, so only its 511
> keV line is comparable at all. The rest of the discussion below stands: the
> bar's "global Wasserstein ≤ v0.7.2's" clause is not currently met, but
> coupling and line-existence are won overwhelmingly.

The whole decision is "is v0.8 better than v0.7.2." The comparison in
[`v0.8_v072_comparison.md`](v0.8_v072_comparison.md) §5 was **qualitative** —
plots by eye, plus the observation that v0.7.2's logE couplings "were never
verified to the ±0.02 standard v0.8 now meets." That gap is now closed (above).

### 5.2 No mechanism for the line-normalization failure — two candidates

Both failed levers (§2.4) assumed the gate needed re-weighting. Two untested
hypotheses are better grounded.

**(a) The gate sees a different `z` at training than at generation.**

The gate is conditioned on the latent (`energy_flow_condition="z_cond"`).
During training it receives `z ~ q(z|x)` from the encoder posterior; during
generation, `z ~ p(z|cond)` from the learned prior. If those differ, the gate
emits different mixing weights — appearing exactly as systematic, per-line,
seed-dependent intensity error.

`val_kl ≈ 21–28` on every run at `latent_dim=8` (~3 nats/dim ≈ 2.4σ
per-dimension displacement) says they *do* differ substantially. Part of this is
structural rather than a defect: `ConditionalCouplingPrior` conditions only on
the particle-type one-hot, so it can express 6 distributions (CR) while `q(z|x)`
varies per event with energy and geometry — see
[`v0.8_learnable_prior_theory.md`](v0.8_learnable_prior_theory.md) §3 for why
this gap damages generation specifically, and §4 for why the *energy* path is
the one that leaks.

**This makes [`v0.8.2_plan.md`](v0.8.2_plan.md) §3 (the KL/latent diagnostic)
not a side quest but a candidate root cause of the beta blocker**, and promotes
it into Phase A.

> **✅ Audited 2026-07-29 — Phase A2.** New
> `tools/gate_posterior_prior_audit.py`; full results in
> [`v0.8.1_line_truth.md`](v0.8.1_line_truth.md) §13.2,
> `docs/_data/gate_posterior_prior_audit.json`. The clean "mismatch vs match"
> fork this section poses did not happen — the answer forks **by source**:
> - **CR: partial confirmation.** Al Kα1, Cu Kα1, Cu Kβ each have a
>   posterior→prior gate-fraction shift whose direction and rough size match
>   their measured recovery error (e.g. Al Kα1: gate ratio 0.448, measured
>   recovery 0.702 — both "under by about half"). The dominant 511 keV line
>   is the exception: its 1.68× over-generation is far larger than its 1.10×
>   gate shift, so something else drives most of that one.
> - **Small: ruled out.** Gate fractions under posterior vs prior are nearly
>   identical (ratios 1.00–1.02) for every slot, including the two
>   catastrophically over-generated lines (3.4×, 4.9×). A process that barely
>   moves the gate cannot be causing a 4.9× error — Small's line problem needs
>   a different mechanism (candidate (b) below, or a gate-training defect).
>
> **Consequence for Phase C:** condition-the-prior (candidate 1) is a
> plausible fix for CR's non-511 lines specifically, but should not be
> expected to help Small — score the two sources separately rather than
> requiring one fix to clear both, consistent with the §4 bar already
> allowing a per-source pass.

**(b) There may be a minimum learnable component fraction.**

Sorting §2.2 by real line fraction:

| fraction | recovery / routing |
|---|---|
| 1.52e-2 | 1.677 |
| 3.95e-3 | 1.232 |
| 3.10e-3 | 0.702 |
| 1.05e-3 | 0.688 |
| 4.20e-4 | 1.166 |
| **5.44e-5** | **3.412** |
| **3.31e-5** | **4.903** |

The two rarest components — both below 1e-4 — are the two catastrophic
over-generations. Above ~4e-4 everything sits within ~1.7×. Asking the gate to
resolve a 3e-5 probability from 62 positive examples may simply be below what is
learnable.

**Confound to respect:** with 7 points, "rarest" and "Small" are almost the same
set. This could be a source effect (Small has 1.87M events and 2 types; CR has
3.44M and 6) rather than a fraction effect. Torio is the natural third source to
disentangle it, and a checkpoint already exists (`trained_models/v0_8_1_Torio/`).

If (b) holds, it is a *methodological result worth publishing* — "MAGI models
lines down to fraction f_min" — not merely a limitation.

### Phase B — Line-set honesty (2 – 8 Aug)

**v0.8.2 §5**, significance threshold at 5σ, replacing `count >= 100` in the
three places listed in that plan. Re-audit CR / Small / Torio.

Expected effect, computed from current counts:

| Line | Significance | At a 5σ floor |
|---|---|---|
| CR 511 | ~228σ | kept |
| CR Al Kα1 | ~103σ | kept |
| CR Cu Kβ | ~38σ | kept |
| CR Cu Kα1 | ~34σ | kept |
| Small 511 | ~86σ | kept |
| Small Al Kα2 | ~10σ | kept |
| **Small Cu Kα1** | **~4.5σ** | **dropped** |

**Be clear about what this does and does not buy.** It converts Small's worst
failure (4.9× over) into an honest "below detection threshold" — the correct
scientific statement for a 62-event feature. It does **not** fix Small's Al Kα2
(10σ, still over-generated 3.4×). §5 is necessary hygiene, not a sufficient fix.

Changes the line set, so scoring needs a retrain: 3 seeds × 2 sources ≈ 10 h,
overnight.

### Phase C — The fix, chosen by Phase A (2 – 13 Aug)

If A2 confirms the posterior/prior mismatch, candidate interventions, cheapest
first:

1. **Condition the prior on more than particle type** (e.g. add the gate-target
   summary or a coarse energy band) so `p(z|cond)` can track `q(z|x)` where the
   lines live.
2. **β annealing** to close the aggregate KL gap directly.
3. **Route the gate off posterior-consistent `z`** at generation time.

One 3-seed A/B each (~10 h), **with a control line deliberately held out** —
the omission that made Small's arm of the gate-weight experiment uninterpretable
([§10.5](v0.8.1_line_truth.md)).

### Phase D — Speed. Conditional. (only if Phase C needs ≥2 A/Bs)

**v0.8.2 §1b**, stratified continuum subsampling (~5×). Break-even is ~1.3
A/Bs, so it pays for itself quickly — but it changes the training distribution
and needs its own equivalence run to prove it. **Not worth the added risk this
close to the deadline unless Phase C is genuinely going to run several
experiments.** Otherwise land it in v0.8.2 proper, after the beta.

### 15 Aug — decide

Score the best candidate on both sources, 3 seeds, against the §4 bar. Then
either the defaults flip ([`v0.8.1_improvement_plan.md`](v0.8.1_improvement_plan.md)
§4, last remaining item) or the fallback (§8).

## 8. Abort criterion and the fallback

**Abort criterion: if Phase A produces no mechanism by 2 Aug, or no Phase C
candidate clears the bar on at least one source by 8 Aug, stop running
experiments.** Both prior tuning efforts consumed days and returned noise; the
discipline that was missing then is a pre-committed stopping point now.

**The fallback requires no code work.** v0.8 is *already* opt-in: the model
constructor and
[`load_task_adaptive_model_for_generation`](../MAGI_package/magi/training/checkpointing.py)
still resolve to the pre-v0.8 architecture by default (`continuum_mode="gaussian"`,
`prior="gaussian"`, `continuum_flow_warp="affine"`), and the v0.8 flags are set
explicitly by the run scripts and notebooks. Shipping "v0.7.2 as beta, v0.8.1 as
experimental" means writing release notes, not changing behaviour.

The paper is unaffected either way. The novel contributions — the conditional
prior preserving energy↔geometry coupling through generation, and sub-bin
detector-resolution line components — are validated and now carry multi-seed
error bars, which is a **stronger** claim than the single-run numbers the
earlier drafts quoted. What changes in the no-go case is only which version
ships as the default.
